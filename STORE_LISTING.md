## Full description (≤4000)

```
Forever Jukebox reimagines the music you already own. Pick a song from your
device and the app analyzes it on the spot, then plays it back as a never-ending,
beat-accurate visual journey that rearranges the track on the fly.

Everything runs locally on your phone. There are no accounts, no ads, and nothing
to buy — just your music and the visualization.

FEATURES

• On-device analysis — choose any audio file and Forever Jukebox detects its beats
  and structure right on your device. Your files never leave your phone.
• Endless playback — the engine jumps between similar moments in the song for a
  seamless, ever-changing mix that never quite ends.
• Beat-accurate visuals — a native visualization engine reacts to every beat, with
  multiple layouts and a fullscreen mode.
• Tuning controls — shape how the visualization and remixing behave.
• Light / dark / system theme.
• Fast reloads — analysis results are cached on-device and can be cleared anytime
  from Settings.

PRIVACY

Forever Jukebox does its work entirely on your device. The app collects no
personal data and only sends anonymous crash diagnostics to help fix bugs. See the
full privacy policy for details.

OPEN SOURCE

Forever Jukebox is free, open-source software (AGPLv3). You can read exactly how it
works and follow development at:
https://github.com/creightonlinza/forever-jukebox-android

NOTES

• Requires Android 8.0 (API 26) or newer.
• Analysis is demanding; devices with less than 4 GB of RAM may struggle with
  longer tracks.
```
